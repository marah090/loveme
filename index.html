For Mami

Something I wrote just for you.

Tap the envelope to open

I have started this letter more times than I can count, and every time I find myself running out of paper before I ever run out of reasons to admire you. So I am going to write it the only way I know how: honestly, with all the sincerity I have in me.

You walked into my life quietly, and somehow everything got brighter. The ordinary things, like a seeing you for far, the sound of your laugh, became my favourite parts of the day. I never knew that a person could feel like ..... until I met you.

You are my calm when the world is loud, and my joy when the day is long.

I don’t have a perfect promise to make, but I have a true one: I will choose you. On the easy days, on the hard days, and on the days when we are both tired. I will listen, I will grow, and I will keep making you smile for as long as you’ll let me.

When I think about the future, I don’t see a grand picture. I see you beside me, laughing at something small. And that is more than enough for me. It is everything.

Mami, I’m grateful to share my life with you.

Forever yours,
Your love ♥
